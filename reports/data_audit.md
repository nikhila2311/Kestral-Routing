## Text quality

**Product conflicts:** 1,744 requests.
Rule: text_clean names at least one other product family and not the row's own product_family (whole-word match on the patterns listed). A word such as "fan" can also mean a part, so some may be false.

- Air Fryer: `fryers?`
- Ceiling Fan: `fans?`
- Induction Cooktop: `cooktops?|induction`
- Mixer Grinder: `mixers?|grinders?|mixies?`
- Robot Vacuum: `vacuums?|robots?`
- Room Heater: `heaters?`
- Water Purifier: `purifiers?|ro`

**Repeated text:** repeats count every row after the first use of a text; overlap counts test rows whose text

## Cost of the bot

Bot transfer and contact cost: **Rs 122,171 per month**,
from 3,902 transfers at Rs 305 plus 2,471 misroutes at Rs 260,
divided by 15 months.

- From misrouted requests only: Rs 114,994 per month.
- 126 misroutes have no recorded transfer, so only their contact
  cost is counted.
- Includes 353 transfers on requests the bot labelled correctly, worth
  Rs 7,178 per month, which a better router may not avoid.
- Excludes the bot licence: Rs 320,000 per year, or
  Rs 26,667 per month.
